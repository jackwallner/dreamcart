# DreamCart Privacy Policy

> Privacy policy for the DreamCart iPhone app by Jack Wallner.

Web page: https://jackwallner.com/ios/dreamcart/privacy-policy.html

## DreamCart Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

DreamCart is a virtual fashion shopping game. It uses an account and a
backend so your game progress can sync between sessions.

## What we collect

- **Account data:** a Supabase account identifier and, when
  you choose to share it through Sign in with Apple, an email address.
- **Game data:** display name, coin balance, closet,
  outfits, favorites, streaks, and in-game purchase history.
- **Product and usage events:** sessions, onboarding steps,
  product views, dwell time, swipes, cart actions, virtual checkouts,
  wheel results, coin-shop views, and coin-pack purchase events. Events
  may include account ID, a temporary session ID, product IDs, coin
  totals, app version, locale, and timestamps. Shipping address contents
  are not included in these events.
- **Simulated checkout details:** if you enter a shipping
  address, it is stored locally for the simulated checkout. DreamCart
  does not ship real goods and does not collect payment card details.

## How we use it

Supabase stores account and game data to operate the game, sync
progress, and support your account. Usage events help us understand
feature performance and improve the game. We do not sell data, use
cross-app advertising, or use your data for behavioral advertising.

## Third-party services

- [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy) provides
  authentication, database hosting, and storage.
- [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/) validates
  coin-pack purchases. It may receive an anonymous app user ID and
  transaction data, not your closet or other gameplay content.
- Apple provides Sign in with Apple and App Store payments. Apple
  receives payment details directly.
- Pollinations provides on-demand AI product and outfit imagery. Prompts
  are generated from catalog or outfit descriptions and do not include
  your identity or account data.

## Retention, deletion, and choices

You can delete your account in the app under You, Delete account. This
deletes your profile and game data from the backend. Account-linked
analytics rows become unlinked when the account is deleted and may be
retained in deidentified form for product and security metrics. We
retain records only as long as needed for the service, security,
support, legal obligations, and transaction reconciliation. Contact us
for a privacy request. Apple and RevenueCat retain purchase records
under their policies.

## Children

DreamCart is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly
collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The date
above identifies the current version.
