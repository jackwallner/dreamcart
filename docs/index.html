How it's built.

The Pitch

Shopping feels good long before the package arrives. Browsing, choosing, filling a cart, checking out, unboxing: that's the loop, and the credit card bill is just the part bolted onto the end.

DreamCart keeps the loop and drops the bill. Coins arrive free every day, the catalog is original virtual fashion, and everything you check out with joins a closet you can style. No merchandise exists, and none is shipped.

Coins

DreamCart is free to play. Coins arrive from the daily drop, the free daily wheel, and streaks.

Optional coin packs. Consumable packs are available through Apple for players who want to shop faster. They are for in-app use only, have no cash value, and are never required to keep playing.

DreamCart+. An optional weekly membership with double daily coins and unlimited wheel spins, starting with a 3-day free trial. Every world, every drop, and the whole closet stay open to free players. Prices are shown in the app and vary by region.

Frequently asked questions

What is a dopamine shopping game?

DreamCart is a dopamine shopping game for iPhone. You browse original virtual fashion, fill a cart, check out with coins and keep everything in a closet, with none of the real spending. No physical items are sold or shipped.

Is there a shopping app where you do not spend real money?

Yes. DreamCart is free to play, and its coins arrive free through the daily drop, the free daily wheel and streaks. Optional coin packs and DreamCart+ exist, but they are for in-app use only and are never required to keep playing.

What can you do in DreamCart?

You can browse original virtual clothing brands across ten style worlds, add items to a cart, check out with coins and open a virtual delivery. Everything you buy joins a closet that you can style into outfits.

Is the clothing real?

No. Every item, brand, coin and delivery in DreamCart is imaginary, and the catalog is original artwork made for the app. Nothing real is sold, shipped or charged, and coins have no cash value.

DreamCart+ is an optional weekly membership with double daily coins and unlimited wheel spins, starting with a free trial. Every world, every drop and the whole closet stay open to free players. Prices are shown in the app and vary by region.

Do I need an account to play?

You sign in with Apple, which gives DreamCart a stable ID and never your email unless you share it. Your coins, closet and outfits sync so progress follows you between sessions, and no shipping address is ever collected.

Is DreamCart a way to stop impulse shopping?

No. DreamCart is a game and not a treatment or a substitute for professional support. It simply lets you enjoy browsing, choosing and checking out without a bill.
